Why We Ship It

Noctis Lucis Caelum is the main protagonist of Final Fantasy XV and Tifa Lockhart is a beloved heroine and major recurring pillar in the Final Fantasy VII series. Even though they hail from separate games in the franchise, they share a striking depth of character, psychological parallels, and complementary strengths.

This space is dedicated to exploring their dynamic, celebrating creative imaginations, and providing a home for fan art, fanfiction, and thoughtful discussion.
